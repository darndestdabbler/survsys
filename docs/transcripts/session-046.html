Work Plan

Session 046 wires outbound CDC notification to real cases and gives it its queue, its reconciliation boundary and its export log. Survsys.Data gains ReconciliationCutoff, a pure readonly record struct over an MMWR year and week -- the shape MmwrWeek has, holding no clock and no DbContext -- whose Counts method answers whether a case at a given MmwrWeek falls within the reconciliation window, strictly after the cutoff so the cutoff week itself is not counted, matching the legacy rule that only records after the boundary enter the verification totals. Survsys.Data gains two entities, each with its own internal IEntityTypeConfiguration mapping to the legacy NMI snake_case table and column names and a DbSet on SurvsysDbContext: NotificationQueueEntry, the outbound queue reduced from the legacy nmi.export_queue -- one row per case, unique on its person-condition id, with the time it was queued and a hold flag -- and CdcExportLogEntry, the append-only export log reduced from the legacy nmi.log -- the person-condition id, the record number, the guide id, the generated message, the queued and sent times, and the outcome with its detail. A single migration, 20261017060000_NotificationQueueAndExportLog, creates both tables with the updated model snapshot, each with a cascade-delete foreign key to person_condition so a deleted case takes its queue entry and log rows with it. Over the queue, Survsys.Data gains CaseNotificationQueue, which handles a CaseChanged domain event by upserting the one queue entry for that case -- inserting it where the case is not yet queued and refreshing the queued time where it is. Survsys.Api raises that CaseChanged event from PersonConditionQueries after each case write -- on creation, status change, assignment and workflow advance -- within the same unit of work as the write, and AddPersonConditions registers the handler, so a case is auto-queued for notification in application code rather than by the legacy database trigger, and this session adds no database trigger of its own. Survsys.UnitTests gains ReconciliationCutoffTests, named after the rule class, proving a case before the cutoff is not counted, one after it is, one on the cutoff week is not, and the year takes precedence over the week across a year boundary. Survsys.IntegrationTests gains CaseNotificationQueueTests, driving the real case endpoints against the database to prove a created case is queued exactly once, that a further change to it refreshes the one entry rather than duplicating it, and that the entry names the case. docs/planning gains netss-export-question.md, which puts to the operator in writing whether CDC still requires the legacy NETSS fixed-width export and records the standing default until they answer -- that it is not built.
