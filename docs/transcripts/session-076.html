Work Plan

Wire vocabulary and rules into ELR intake. Survsys.Data gains the remaining vocabulary tiers as data: a master value table (vocab_master_vocab: a category of specimen or test_result, the master concept, and the application value Survsys records for it), a lab-specific child code table (vocab_child_vocab: a sending facility, a category of specimen, test_result or organism, the lab's code, and the master value or master organism it resolves to), and the condition's rules (vocab_rules and vocab_rule_conditions: a message-filter or case-management rule on a condition, its execution order, the allow-new-case, allow-update-case and surveillance flags, and AND/OR conditions in the shape ExpressionEvaluator reads); a staged message gains its condition, investigating agency and the three case-management flags, and each staged result its master LOINC, condition, organism and interpretation; a Filtered review state holds a message its condition's filter refused, with the migration. Survsys.Messaging's VocabularyMatcher resolves a result through the child tier (child LOINC, child organism, child specimen and result codes for the sending lab), the master tier (master LOINC, SNOMED organism, master value) and the application tier (the master value's application value), taking the organism from the result's SNOMED when the master LOINC says so; a new IntakeRules evaluates a condition's message-filter rules (none defined is an exception, any matching accepts, none matching filters, a rule reading a field the message lacks is an exception) and its case-management rules (exactly one must match, or the message is held with why) through ExpressionEvaluator. Survsys.Api's IntakeTranslator applies all of that to each staged message the intake queues, picks the investigating agency from the patient's zip through ZipAgencyRules, and holds as an exception a message whose results match no vocabulary; ElrIntakeService.Receive loads the vocabulary, rules and zip mapping and runs it for both transports. A sending facility's id is the lab id the child tiers are keyed by. Survsys.Api gains /api/vocabulary endpoints listing, creating, updating and deleting master and child LOINC, master organisms, master values and child codes, each with CSV export and import, and /api/conditions/{id}/rules to read and replace a condition's rules; Survsys.Web gains a Vocabulary screen with a tab per table and the lab-specific overrides beside their masters, CSV export and import on each, and the rules editor on the condition's screen. Integration tests replay the legacy cases from EMSA_VOCABULARY_SYSTEM.md and EMSA_RULES_ENGINE.md.
