GraylistRules is the time-windowed duplicate detection: given a new event's request (its condition and event date) and the waiting graylisted candidates (each a condition and a reference date) and a window of whole days before and after, a candidate matches when its condition equals the request's and its reference date falls inside the window around the event date. PersonMatchRules is the person matching, fuzzy and exact: it classifies a candidate carrying an EpiTrax match score into Exact, Fuzzy, or None by the tiered score thresholds, promoting a fuzzy-band candidate to Exact on a shared medical record number and demoting one with no date of birth to None. Survsys.UnitTests gains GraylistRulesTests and PersonMatchRulesTests, named after the two rule classes, and the graylist tests cover a candidate just inside and just outside the window.

Graylist rule

Survsys.Messaging: GraylistRules, the time-windowed duplicate detection, pure and taking its inputs as plain in-memory records so the module keeps its fence against Survsys.Data and Entity Framework Core and references neither. In GraylistRules.cs define, as small immutable records and none of them a Survsys.Data entity: GraylistRequest (a new event's ConditionId and its EventDate as a DateOnly), GraylistCandidate (a waiting graylisted message's ConditionId and its ReferenceDate as a DateOnly), and GraylistWindow (DaysBefore and DaysAfter as whole-day ints). Give GraylistWindow a sensible named default. Then the static GraylistRules class: a method that tells whether one candidate matches a request within a window -- its ConditionId equals the request's and its ReferenceDate falls on or within the window, that is on or after EventDate minus DaysBefore and on or before EventDate plus DaysAfter -- and a method that selects, from a sequence of candidates, those that match. The window is inclusive at both bounds, so a candidate exactly on a bound matches and one a day past it does not. Guard the sequence and the records against null. Write no test in this step.

Person match rule

Survsys.Messaging: PersonMatchRules, the person matching fuzzy and exact, pure and taking its inputs as plain in-memory records so the module keeps its fence against Survsys.Data and Entity Framework Core. In PersonMatchRules.cs define a PersonMatchKind enum (Exact, Fuzzy, None), a PersonMatchCandidate record (the candidate's PersonId, its EpiTrax match Score as a double from 0 to 100, its DateOfBirth as a nullable DateOnly, and its MedicalRecordNumbers as a read-only string collection), and the static PersonMatchRules class with the two score thresholds as named constants (auto-match at 95 and no-match at 70). Its Classify method takes a candidate and the incoming message's medical record numbers and returns a PersonMatchKind: a score at or above the auto-match threshold is Exact and a score below the no-match threshold is None; within the fuzzy band between them, a medical record number shared with the incoming message (compared case-insensitively) promotes to Exact, a candidate with no date of birth is None, and anything else is Fuzzy. Guard the inputs against null. Write no test in this step.
