Work Plan

Give survsys its dynamic form builder across Survsys.Data, Survsys.Api and Survsys.Web: condition-specific supplemental forms built from nested sections and questions whose answers are free text or a coded choice, assigned to a case and completed against it, and auto-assigned when a case is opened for the condition a form belongs to. Survsys.Data gains Form (a named supplemental form that belongs to one condition and may be marked for auto-assignment), FormSection (a titled, ordered section of a form), FormQuestion (an ordered question in a section, answered as text or a code, carrying its required flag), FormQuestionOption (the allowed choices of a coded question), FormAssignment (a form given to a case) and FormAnswer (one answer to one question of an assignment), each with its configuration, all in one migration. Survsys.Api gains the /api/forms slice: the form builder (create, read and save a form with its sections, questions and options), a case's assigned forms with their current answers, saving a case's answers with coded answers validated against the question's options and required questions enforced, and auto-assignment of a condition's auto-assign forms when a new case is created for that condition. Survsys.Web gains the form builder screen (a forms list and a form editor that builds the nested sections, questions and coded options) and the form-completion screen (a Forms tab on the case that lists the case's assigned forms and records the answers). The schema is a lean reading of EpiTrax's form, form_element, question, answer, form_reference and condition_form_auto tables, holding only what a condition-specific supplemental form, its assignment to a case and its answers need.

Form entities

Add the form domain to Survsys.Data as entities and their IEntityTypeConfiguration files, and nothing of the migration yet. Form is the supplemental form under the legacy form table kept lean: id, name (required, as a dropdown and a list show it), description (nullable), condition_id (FK to conditions, no cascade, required — a form is condition-specific), auto_assign (bool, default false — whether it is assigned to a case automatically when the condition is selected), created_at. FormSection is a titled section under the legacy form_element of type section: id, form_id (FK to forms, cascade on delete), title (required), sort_order (int). FormQuestion is a question in a section under the legacy form_element/question pair: id, form_section_id (FK to form_sections, cascade), text (required, the legacy question_text), help_text (nullable), answer_type (int, default 0 — 0 text, 1 coded), is_required (bool, default false), sort_order (int). FormQuestionOption is a coded question's allowed choice under the legacy answer code set: id, form_question_id (FK to form_questions, cascade), code (required), label (required), sort_order (int). FormAssignment is a form given to a case under the legacy form_reference: id, form_id (FK to forms, no cascade), person_condition_id (FK to person_conditions, cascade on delete), assigned_at, unique on (form_id, person_condition_id) so a form is assigned once to a case. FormAnswer is one answer under the legacy answer: id, form_assignment_id (FK to form_assignments, cascade), form_question_id (FK to form_questions, no cascade), text_answer (nullable), code (nullable), created_at, unique on (form_assignment_id, form_question_id) so a question is answered once per assignment. Register each new entity as a DbSet on SurvsysDbContext. Follow Outbreak and OutbreakConfiguration for the entity-plus-configuration shape, the legacy column names, and the XML-doc grounding in the legacy table and its source file (legacy/nedss-jpa/src/main/resources/db/migration/V0001__create.sql under ../nedss); configurations stay internal sealed.

Non-goals:

  • No condition_form / condition_form_auto join tables and none of the legacy form machinery beyond a lean core: a form names its one condition directly with an auto-assign flag rather than through a many-to-many join, sections contain questions one level deep rather than through the legacy form_element lft/rgt nested-set tree, and there is no template, version, rollback, agency-scoping, short-name or question-bank support (legacy form.is_template, template_id, version, status, rolled_back_from_id, form_agency, question_bank).
  • No exposure or outbreak form data (legacy answer.exposure_id, answer.outbreak_id, form_reference.exposure_id, form_reference.outbreak_id): a form is assigned to and answered against a case (person_condition) only this session, not against an exposure or an outbreak.
  • Auto-assignment runs on the new-case creation path only (PersonConditionEndpoints), assigning the condition's auto-assign forms when the case is created; it does not retroactively assign to existing cases, does not fire on the lab-intake or contact-import case-creation paths, and does not re-assign when a case's condition changes afterward.
  • No export mapping of answers (legacy export_column_id, export_conversion_value_id) and no core-data binding (legacy question.core_data, core_path): answers are stored as entered, not projected to NETSS/CDC columns and not bound to core elements.
  • No treatments or vaccine records (those are session 53), and no release or packaging: this session ships application code only.
