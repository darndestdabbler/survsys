The operator decided on 2026-10-23 that an address is geocoded by the US Census Geocoder, falling back to Google's Geocoding API when the Census Geocoder fails or finds no match.*

Non-goals. No test, check or shipped configuration calls either real service: both are exercised against local stubs, and the shipped configuration leaves geocoding off. No map, and no geocoding of exposure places, which already carry their own coordinates.
